Enhancing Memory Retention Through AR-Based Learning:
A Study on the Gas Turbine Engine Module Among First-Year Aircraft Maintenance Technology Students at Daniel P. Rivera Aviation College

Project Context

In response to the rapidly advancing field of aviation technology, this project explores the transformative potential of Augmented Reality (AR) in aviation education. Focusing on first-year Aircraft Maintenance Technology (AMT) students at Daniel P. Rivera Aviation College, the study leverages AR to deliver immersive, interactive learning experiences on the complex gas turbine engine module. By integrating AR with traditional teaching methods, the project aims to boost student engagement, deepen conceptual understanding, and significantly enhance long-term memory retention. Grounded in Mayer's Cognitive Theory of Multimedia Learning, this research pioneers the use of cutting-edge AR tools to prepare future aircraft technicians for real-world challenges, ensuring safety, precision, and operational excellence in aviation maintenance.
